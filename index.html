About us

Who we are

HERA Initiative is a youth-focused Community-Based Organization committed to promoting Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights through education, advocacy, community engagement, and referrals.

We work directly with adolescents, pregnant teens, pregnant young mothers, and breastfeeding young mothers — helping them make informed choices and lead healthier lives through accessible knowledge, compassionate support, and referrals to youth-friendly services.

Every programme we run is rooted in dignity: no young person navigating pregnancy or motherhood should have to do it without information, without support, or without rights.

Respect Equity Integrity Accountability Inclusion

Vision

Mission

To promote Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights by empowering adolescents, pregnant teens, pregnant young mothers, and breastfeeding young mothers through education, advocacy, and community support.

Our programs

Support that follows the journey

From the first questions of adolescence through pregnancy and into motherhood — our programmes meet young people at each stage.

01

Stage one

Adolescent SRHR

  • Comprehensive sexuality education
  • Menstrual health
  • HIV & STI prevention
  • Healthy relationships
  • Family planning information and referrals
02

Stage two

Pregnant Teens Support

  • Pregnancy education
  • Antenatal support
  • Birth preparedness
  • Counselling and referrals
03

Stage three

Pregnant & Breastfeeding Young Mothers

  • Maternal health education
  • Breastfeeding support
  • Newborn care education
  • Postnatal family planning
  • Peer support
